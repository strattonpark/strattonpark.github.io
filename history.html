Early History

The area was originally associated with the Siwanoy people and later Dutch and English settlements in Westchester.

Development

Mapes family lands were subdivided into Park Versailles. Later, John Stratton O'Leary developed apartment buildings that gave the neighborhood its identity.

Golden Age

Local churches, businesses, clubs, and family life made Stratton Park a vibrant community for decades.
